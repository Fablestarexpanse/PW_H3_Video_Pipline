# Tansy — the cook

| Axis | Words |
|---|---|
| face | round-faced dormouse, bright dark eyes, a flour smudge on one cheek |
| build | small, stout |
| hair | warm russet fur, a bun of whiskers tied with twine |
| skin | pink nose, paws stained with berry juice |
| age | middle-aged |
| wardrobe | rolled cream linen sleeves, a long ochre apron over a brown dress, wooden clogs |

Signature: the apron and the berry-stained paws. Runs the great kitchen; feeds the abbey and counts what is left.
Role in the pilot: the one who notices the stranger is hungry before anyone notices he is armed.
