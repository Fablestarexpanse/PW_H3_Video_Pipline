## Premise
Recurring-character short-form reels, not a narrative film: one fixed character (Wren) in one fixed location (a boho
sunroom) performs a repeating "build a [theme] outfit with me" format across rotating themes. Shot like a phone on a
locked central axis; she performs to the lens. The fixed axis is what makes garment changes read as polished.
Independent short Ref2VA generations (124-226 frames) cut together; accessory/fabric occlusions hide the seams. No LoRA.
